What are Crest 3D White Strips?

Crest teeth whitening strips are thin, flexible strips coated with a peroxide-based whitening gel. They are designed to be applied to your teeth for 30 minutes while the peroxide gel gets to work whitening your teeth.

How long does it take to see results?

Always a common question. Most people start to see results within a few days, normally after 3 treatments. However, this depends on how well you use them, for how long, and whether you’re applying them correctly.

Tips for getting the best results with Crest White Strips

✓Follow the instructions

Follow the instructions on the package carefully. Don’t leave them on overnight, for example.

✓Use them for the recommended time

Use the strips for the recommended amount of time.

✕Avoid staining food and drink

Avoid eating or drinking anything that could stain your teeth while the strips are on: coffee, wine, etc.

✓Keep your teeth clean

Brush and floss your teeth regularly, and do this properly before the whitening treatment to make sure no food or plaque is blocking the whitening gel from your tooth surface.
