Best Teeth Whitening Strips in the UK: Top Picks Compared

By Advanced Whitening · 5 April 2025

Nowadays we are inundated with choice, and the teeth whitening industry is no different. So many brands claim to change your smile and have the best-in-class product. So how do you choose the best one? We’ve compared the key brands on what matters, and one stands out.

The science behind teeth whitening strips

Teeth whitening strips like Crest work by using hydrogen peroxide, a bleaching agent that gets to work on your tooth enamel. It breaks down stains and lightens the overall tooth colour for a pearly smile.

What to consider when choosing a whitening brand

Hydrogen peroxide concentration

Higher concentrations get you faster results but can make your teeth temporarily sensitive if not used as instructed.

Application time and frequency

How long you leave them on for, and how consistent you are. It’s a routine, a course, and you need to stick to it.

Comfort and adhesion

Some whitening treatments have you drooling all over the living room. Well-fitting strips ensure even whitening and minimise discomfort.

Sensitivity levels

Look for strips designed for sensitive teeth if you experience discomfort. The Crest Glamorous White range is great for this.

Brand reputation

Choose reputable brands with good reviews.

Value for money

Check the cost per treatment and see how they weigh up.

Tips for maximising your whitening results

✓Follow the instructions precisely

Don’t go and leave strips on overnight, for example!

✓Brush and floss

Brush and floss regularly to remove plaque and debris.

✕Limit tannin-rich food and drink

Reduce your consumption of coffee, tea, red wine, and other staining foods and drinks.

Which strips are most effective and safe?

Effectiveness and safety are important factors to take into account when choosing teeth whitening strips. For proven, professional-level results with an enamel-safe formula, our pick is Crest 3D White Whitestrips Professional Effects Luxe Edition. Peroxide-free options like charcoal or dissolving strips may suit people looking for a gentler approach, but they work on surface stains only. If you have any concerns regarding sensitivity or oral health, always follow the manufacturer’s recommendations and seek advice from a dentist.
