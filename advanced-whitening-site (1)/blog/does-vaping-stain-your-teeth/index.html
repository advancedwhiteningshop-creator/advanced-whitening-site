Does Vaping Stain Your Teeth? (How to Fix Them)

By Advanced Whitening · 1 June 2025

If you’ve made the switch from smoking to vaping, you might think your teeth are safe from stains, but that’s not quite the case. While vaping doesn’t contain tar like cigarettes, it can still affect the colour and health of your teeth over time.

In this guide, we’ll look at how vaping causes stains and what you can do to fix and prevent them.

Can vaping stain your teeth?

Vaping is often seen as a “cleaner” alternative to smoking, but it can still stain your teeth. Unlike cigarette smoke, vaping doesn’t contain tar, but that doesn’t mean your teeth are in the clear.

Vape juice often contains nicotine, which can yellow your teeth over time. Propylene glycol, another common ingredient, dries out your mouth. This lack of saliva makes it easier for stains to stick.

Vape stains tend to build up slowly. They’re usually less brown than tobacco stains but still dull the natural whiteness of your teeth. Over time, this can make your smile look less bright, especially if you vape regularly.

How vaping affects oral health

Vaping can lead to dry mouth, which means your mouth doesn’t make enough saliva. Saliva helps wash away food and bacteria, so without it, plaque can build up faster and increase the risk of tooth decay.

Your gums can also take a hit. Vaping may cause inflammation and reduce blood flow. This will make your gums more prone to infection and even receding over time.

If you vape regularly and don’t stay on top of your oral hygiene, the long-term risks grow. You could face enamel erosion, gum disease, and even tooth loss.

What do vape stains look like?

Vape stains usually show up as a yellowish tint that builds up slowly over time. You might not notice it at first, but your teeth can start to look dull or less white than usual.

The front teeth are often the most affected since they’re the most exposed during vaping. These are also the teeth people see when you smile, so the stains can be quite noticeable.

Compared to coffee, tea, or tobacco, vape stains are usually lighter and more gradual. They’re less likely to leave dark brown patches like smoking does, but they can still make your smile look tired and less bright over time.

How to remove vape stains from teeth

✓Keep up good daily habits

Good daily habits make a big difference. Brush twice a day with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and drink plenty of water to keep your mouth clean and stop plaque from building up.

✓Use whitening toothpaste or mouthwash

Whitening toothpaste or mouthwash can help lift light stains and keep your teeth looking brighter. They won’t work overnight but can help over time if you use them regularly.

✓Try whitening strips

For a stronger option, try teeth whitening strips like Crest 3D Whitestrips. They’re easy to use at home and can noticeably whiten teeth in just a few days.

How to prevent vape stains in the future

✓Cut down on nicotine

Cutting down on nicotine, or avoiding it altogether, can make a big difference. It’s one of the main ingredients that causes yellowing, so using low-nicotine or nicotine-free vape juice can help protect your teeth.

✓Stay hydrated

Staying hydrated is also key. Drinking plenty of water keeps your mouth from drying out, which helps prevent plaque and stains from building up.

✕Don’t skip dental check-ups

Seeing your dentist regularly means any early signs of staining or damage can be spotted and treated before they get worse. A professional clean also helps keep your smile bright.

Should you be worried about vaping and teeth?

Vaping might not stain as badly as smoking, but it still comes with risks. These include discolouration, dry mouth, and gum problems.

Brushing, staying hydrated, and using whitening products can all help keep your smile looking fresh. Cutting back on nicotine and seeing your dentist regularly also makes a big difference.
