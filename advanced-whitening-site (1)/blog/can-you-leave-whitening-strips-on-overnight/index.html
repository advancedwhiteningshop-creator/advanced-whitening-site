Can You Leave Whitening Strips On Overnight? (What You Need to Know)

By Advanced Whitening · 16 March 2025

Teeth whitening strips are one of the most effective at-home teeth whitening options. But if you don’t follow the recommended guidelines, they can leave you with an overly sensitive, painful smile.

One of the most important guidelines is the duration. Leaving whitening strips on for too long (or even overnight) can bring sensitivity to your once pain-free mouth. So, in short, no, you can’t leave whitening strips on overnight.

But let’s dive into the details so you can truly understand why.

How whitening strips work

Teeth whitening strips are made from a flexible material and coated in carbamide peroxide or hydrogen peroxide. The former becomes hydrogen peroxide when water touches it. These chemicals whiten and brighten your smile when left on your teeth for the right period of time.

The question is: how?

Well, once applied to your teeth, the peroxides penetrate the outer layer (called enamel) and travel to the deeper dentin layer. Once there, they bleach the chromogens (pigmented molecules found inside and outside the tooth that cause staining), transforming them into the pearly whites you want them to be.

What happens if you leave whitening strips on overnight?

While the peroxides we’ve just mentioned are the active ingredients that make whitening strips so effective, they are also the substances that can cause problems if you leave your whitening strips on overnight.

Since they reach the dentin layer of your teeth, leaving them on for too long can cause nerve irritation, the key culprit for increased tooth sensitivity. Whether you’re a lover of cold drinks and ice cream or not, having sensitive teeth isn’t something you want.

On top of that, wearing whitening strips for prolonged periods increases your chances of gum irritation and tooth damage. Peroxides are notorious for causing erosion and demineralisation if they’re used excessively. As such, it’s vital that you follow the recommended usage times on the packaging.

What to do if you accidentally leave whitening strips on overnight

The good news is that if you do accidentally leave whitening strips on overnight, the damage isn’t likely to be permanent. However, that doesn’t make the immediate unpleasant (and super painful) feeling any easier to deal with. Luckily, you can help your teeth return to their normal, pain-free selves in just a couple of steps.

✓Use a remineralisation gel

Firstly, use a remineralisation gel to rebuild your teeth’s strength. All you need to do is smear it over your teeth and let it sit for as long as it states on the packaging.

✓Apply a sensitive toothpaste

After that, simply smear a sensitive toothpaste on your teeth and let it sit there for around 15 minutes. It will plug your dental tubules again, so food and drink don’t reach your nerve endings so easily, resulting in less sensitive teeth. While it isn’t necessarily a foolproof cure, it can ease the symptoms after accidentally falling asleep with your whitening strips on.

How long should you leave whitening strips on?

You should always check the packaging of your specific whitening strips to learn how long to leave them on. Every product is different, after all. For Crest 3D White LUXE strips, it’s 30 minutes per session. This balances good results with a lower risk of tooth sensitivity.

We can’t emphasise this enough: always read the packaging for specific timings to get the results and comfort you want. As you now know, leaving them on overnight (or longer than instructed) can cause sensitivity, nerve irritation, and even tooth damage.

Safe alternatives for overnight whitening

If you’re wondering whether you can leave teeth whitening strips on overnight, then you’re probably searching for safe but effective ways to quickly brighten your smile. Whitening strips aren’t the answer to that, but there are a few methods you can try in the evening:

✓Whitening toothpaste and mouthwash

Use a whitening toothpaste and a mouthwash containing hydrogen peroxide before bed to gradually lift stains overnight.

✓Whitening gel trays

Custom-fitted trays with a mild whitening gel can safely whiten teeth while you sleep.

✓Hydration and saliva production

Staying hydrated before bed can prevent staining by promoting saliva production, which naturally cleans teeth.

✕Avoid staining food and drink in the evening

Avoid staining foods and drinks like coffee, red wine, and dark sauces in the evening to stop new discolouration while you sleep.

Can you leave whitening strips on overnight?

While accidentally leaving your whitening strips on overnight isn’t the end of the world, it’s certainly uncomfortable. So, always follow the recommended usage to keep your mouth pain-free, and if an accident happens, use a sensitive toothpaste and remineralisation gel to recover.
