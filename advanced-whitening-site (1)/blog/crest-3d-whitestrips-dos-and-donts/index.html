Crest 3D Whitestrips: Do’s and Don’ts for a Brighter Smile

By Hammad Madjilani · 25 June 2025

Crest 3D Whitestrips are regarded as one of the best at-home whitening solutions in the world. Crest 3D Whitestrips can deliver professional-level whitening in the comfort of your own home. However, it is essential that you use them properly.

This is a guide to the dos and don’ts of Crest 3D Whitestrips. This ensures that you use them properly, delivering the best possible results.

What are Crest 3D Whitestrips?

Crest 3D Whitestrips are an at-home teeth whitening solution. They are small strips coated with a gel containing peroxide. The peroxide will penetrate deep into your teeth, helping to reduce the appearance of stains. Crest 3D Whitestrips can remove almost 14 years’ worth of stains.

Assuming they are used correctly, Crest 3D Whitestrips are 100% safe to use, and are among the most effective whitening strip solutions on the market.

Do’s when using Crest 3D Whitestrips

Let’s start with a few tips on how you can use the Crest 3D Whitestrips correctly. This will ensure they work properly, giving you whiter teeth, faster.

✓Do follow the instructions carefully

We know it can seem boring to do but read the instructions that came with your Crest 3D Whitestrips. They’ll tell you exactly how to use the product. This means how often you should use them, anything you should be aware of, etc.

99% of problems people have with their whitening strips can often be solved by reading the instructions properly. Even if you’ve used whitening strips before, there is no guarantee that Crest 3D Whitestrips will work the same way. Take a couple of minutes to read them, and you’ll save yourself a whole load of hassle.

✓Do use on clean, dry teeth

Make sure your teeth are clean and dry, but don’t brush immediately before applying the strips. If you brush, wait about 30 minutes first. You don’t want anything to hamper the whitening process. Even a small food particle stuck on your teeth can stop the bleaching process in certain areas. This can lead to uneven whitening.

✓Do be consistent

You can’t just whiten your teeth once and be done with it. Proper whitening takes time, and you need to be consistent. This doesn’t mean that you should be using the whitening strips constantly (you should always follow the instructions on how often to use them), but you need to have a proper whitening regime in place.

If you are consistent, then you’ll see far, far better results. We promise you.

✓Do store properly

Store your whitening strips in a cool, dry location. So, something like your kitchen cupboards or a medicine cabinet. Not storing the whitening strips properly can reduce their effectiveness.

✓Do check for tooth sensitivity

If you know you’ve got sensitive teeth, then make sure you ‘test’ out the strips on a small section of your mouth beforehand. Because of the way teeth whitening strips work, they can result in serious pain if you have a lot of sensitivity. So, if you often struggle eating or drinking hot or cold items, then be wary.

A small amount of sensitivity is expected, at least the first few times you use the strips. The problem tends to clear up quickly, though. It is just the nerves in your teeth getting slightly irritated. There are only major problems if the tooth pain is constant for longer periods.

Don’ts when using Crest 3D Whitestrips

Now, let’s move on to the things that you really should be avoiding when you use the Crest 3D Whitestrips.

✕Don’t overuse them

More isn’t better. Crest 3D White LUXE strips are designed to be used once a day for 30 minutes, for 20 days. Don’t use more than one set at a time or leave them on longer than directed, as it won’t speed things up and can cause sensitivity.

✕Don’t eat while wearing them

Eating will knock the strips off your teeth, and we can’t imagine it would taste particularly pleasant either. The Advanced Seal grip lets you sip water while you whiten, but save food and other drinks until afterwards. You’ll only have the strips on for 30 minutes, so we’re sure you can hold out.

✕Don’t use on dental work

Dental work can cause light cracks or holes in the teeth. It can also increase tooth sensitivity. Normally, it isn’t something that you’ll need to worry about. However, if you’re using teeth whitening strips, don’t touch the dental work. It may cause you a bit of pain. It may also weaken the dental work which, obviously, you don’t want to have happen!

✕Don’t use if you have oral health issues

Gum pain? Cavities? Random bleeding in your mouth? Don’t use the teeth whitening strips until those issues have been sorted. You’ll just be making your problems a lot worse and, honestly, if you have those issues then how white your teeth are will be the least of your problems.

✕Don’t expect instant results

Many people start using teeth whitening strips thinking that they’ll get instant results. You won’t. You might notice a small amount of stain lightening during the first use, but those stains won’t magically disappear. You’ll probably need to use the whitening strips 3–5 times before you notice any major changes. We promise you, they’ll come. You just need to be patient. Once again, don’t use the strips too frequently. It won’t benefit you in any way. It won’t make the process faster, but it could cause teeth sensitivity issues.

Crest 3D Whitestrips: do’s and don’ts

Follow this advice, and using your Crest 3D Whitestrips should be easy. Just make sure that you read all the instructions included with your strips, and you’ll be just a few weeks away from the smile you’ve always dreamed of!
