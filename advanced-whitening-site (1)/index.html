Description

Crest 3D Whitestrips Professional Effects remove 14 years of stains for a brilliantly whiter smile. You’ll see a whiter smile after 3 treatments (three days), and full results after 20 treatments (40 strips), or 20 days.

The no-slip grip means the strips stay put until you take them off, so you can talk and even drink water while whitening. FlexFit technology lets the strips stretch to cover 30% more of your smile than a non-flexing strip.

1 pouch = 1 treatment. Each pouch contains two strips: one for the top teeth and one for the bottom. Packaging may vary; the product is the same.

How to use
  • Prep: don’t brush immediately before applying. If you brush, wait about 30 minutes first. The strips stick best to clean, dry teeth.
  • Apply: place the lower strip along your gum line and mould it gently. Repeat with the upper strip, folding it so it’s snug on your teeth.
  • Wait 30 minutes: avoid eating while the formula works.
  • Remove: peel off the strips, then rinse or brush away any remaining gel.
  • Repeat: once a day for 20 days. The full retail box (20 pouches) is one complete course.
Ingredients
  • Hydrogen peroxide: tackles surface and deeper stains.
  • Glycerin: helps the strip fit securely to your teeth.
  • Carbomer: works with the hydrogen peroxide to enhance whitening.
  • PEG-8: spreads the whitening gel across every tooth.
  • Sodium hydroxide: sets the right pH for whitening.
  • Sodium saccharin: keeps the taste pleasant.
  • PVP: helps keep the strips firmly in place.

FAQs

Good questions

Can Whitestrips damage my teeth or enamel?

When used as directed, Crest Whitestrips have been shown in clinical trials to be safe for teeth, using an enamel-safe ingredient many dentists use.

Can I use more than 2 sets a day?

No. We do not recommend using more than 2 upper and 2 lower strips in a day.

Can I wear them overnight?

No. Don't wear Whitestrips overnight or while sleeping. Longer wear can cause sensitivity or gum discomfort.

Can I brush before or after?

Don't brush immediately before applying. After using Whitestrips, you can brush gently.

Will I pay customs or extra fees?
